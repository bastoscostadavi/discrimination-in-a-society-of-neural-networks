\section{Observables}
\label{sec:observables}

Two per-pair quantities, both signed and both bounded, and both independent of $K$
and of the architecture --- deliberately, so that the same measurements apply to
any population of agents that can be asked what it thinks and whom it trusts.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Opinion alignment} $\rho_{IJ} = \cos(\wv_I, \wv_J) \in [-1, 1]$.
  \item \textbf{Trust} $\eta_{e|r} = 1 - 2\Phi(\hmu) \in [-1,1]$, equal to $+1$ for
    a fully trusted source and $-1$ for a fully distrusted one. Note that $\eta$
    is directional: $\eta_{I|J} \neq \eta_{J|I}$ in general.
\end{itemize}

With $G_{IJ} = \kappa_I \kappa_J$ the signed label agreement, the three pair
correlations are, averaged over unordered pairs and each normalised to $[-1,1]$,
\begin{equation}
  \Rwm = \bigl\langle (\eta_{I|J} + \eta_{J|I})\,\rho_{IJ}\bigr\rangle,
  \qquad
  \Rmc = \bigl\langle G_{IJ}(\eta_{I|J} + \eta_{J|I})\bigr\rangle,
  \qquad
  \Rcw = \bigl\langle G_{IJ}\,\rho_{IJ}\bigr\rangle .
  \label{eq:correlations}
\end{equation}
$\Rmc$ --- how strongly trust tracks the group label --- is the
\textbf{discrimination order parameter}, and the one the primary claim is about.
$\Rcw$ says whether the split is also visible in what agents believe, and
separating the two is what distinguishes the regimes of \S\ref{sec:phase}.

\paragraph{Balance.} Correlations do not distinguish an ordered population from a
disordered one. Over triples,
\begin{equation}
  \Brho = \bigl\langle \rho_{IJ}\rho_{JK}\rho_{KI}\bigr\rangle,
  \qquad
  \Bet = \Bigl\langle \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(\eta_{IJ}\eta_{JK}\eta_{KI}
        + \eta_{JI}\eta_{IK}\eta_{KJ}\bigr)\Bigr\rangle ,
  \label{eq:balance}
\end{equation}
the second symmetrised because $\eta$ is directional. Two coherent, mutually
opposed blocs give $B = 1$ whatever they are made of; a random population gives
$0$; a population that cannot settle goes negative. $\Brho$ measures balance in
the opinion sector and $\Bet$ in the trust sector, and reading them as a
trajectory in the $(\Brho, \Bet)$ plane is what \S\ref{sec:agenda} does.

Both aggregates have closed forms --- for any $M$ with unit diagonal,
$\sum \! M_{IJ}M_{JK}M_{KI} = \mathrm{tr}(M^3) - 3(\sum_{IJ} M_{IJ}M_{JI} - N) - N$
over distinct ordered triples --- which replaces an $O(N^3)$ enumeration by two
matrix products.

\paragraph{Naming.} The source draft calls these $B_I$ (``ideological'') and $B_A$
(``affective''). We name them after the quantity each is built from, and we do not
use ``ideological'' or ``affective'' anywhere: the $\wv$ sector holds a
classifier and the $\mu$ sector holds a reliability estimate.
