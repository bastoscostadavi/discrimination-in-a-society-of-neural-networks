\paragraph{On $K = 30$.} This is recovered rather than chosen. The source draft's
trajectory figure is labelled
$\alpha = P/K \in \{0.03, 0.17, 0.23, 0.33, 0.50, 0.67, 1.67, 3.33, 333.33\}$,
which are exactly $P/30$ for $P \in \{1, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 50, 100, 10^4\}$.

\paragraph{On $\Delta t$.} The source draft leaves the measurement time as a
literal placeholder. Because the dynamics anneals rather than equilibrating
(\S\ref{sec:rule}), this is not a detail: no trajectory or map in the paper is
meaningful without it. State it, state how it was chosen, and show that the
qualitative conclusions are stable across a range of it.
\todo{a small panel sweeping $\Delta t$ over a decade, showing region assignment is
unchanged, forecloses the obvious objection at negligible cost.}

\paragraph{Seeds.} \needsrun{every map in \S\ref{sec:phase} is currently a single
realisation, which is visible in the pixel noise. Repeat at $\geq 5$ seeds, report
the mean, and put error bars on the extracted boundary of \S\ref{sec:boundary}.
This is cheap and its absence is expensive.}

\paragraph{Deviations from a literal reading of the source draft.} Three, each
made because the literal reading does not work, and each worth stating rather than
silently adopting:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Sign of the perturbation.} See App.~\ref{app:variants}.
  \item \textbf{The class indicator.} The source defines $G_{IJ} \in \{0,1\}$, but
    its own $\Rmc$ map spans $[-1,1]$, which a $\{0,1\}$ indicator cannot reach ---
    under perfect reverse discrimination it returns $-(N/2-1)/(N-1)$, and its range
    depends on $N$. Since $d < 0$ is half the phase diagram, we use the signed
    $G_{IJ} = \kappa_I \kappa_J$.
  \item \textbf{Normalisation of $\Rcw$.} The source normalises all three
    correlations by $N(N-1)$. That is right for the two that sum
    $\eta_{I|J} + \eta_{J|I}$ over unordered pairs, but $\Rcw$ has one term per
    pair and the same divisor caps it at $1/2$. We use $2/(N(N-1))$ there.
\end{enumerate}
